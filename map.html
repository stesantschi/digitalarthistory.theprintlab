Map of the site

The modules lie along one road, and each builds on the ones before it. The guides branch off it, and the reference pages stand beside it.

The Japanese material

The map is laid out like an Edo-period route map (dōchūzu 道中図): one road drawn as a band, with its post stations in order and side roads branching off. Such maps record sequence and connection rather than measured distance.

The start square carries 始, "beginning".

The map in words

  • Start where your question is: sorts the modules by what you want to do.
  • 01 Data literacy: no prerequisite.
  • 02 Images and metadata: requires module 1.
  • 03 Spatial research design: requires modules 1 and 2.
  • 04 Critical computer vision: requires modules 1 to 3.
  • 05 Linked data: requires modules 1 to 4.
  • 06 Display and the book: requires modules 1 and 2.
  • Guides: Models, maps and counting, before the modules; Identifying a book you cannot read, alongside module 2.
  • Reference: Glossary and References, used by every page.
